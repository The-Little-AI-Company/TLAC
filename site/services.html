How booking works

Three steps. The half hour can be booked right now. Everything else starts with an email.

  1. Pick a session

    Pay for a half hour now, or email me for anything longer or for a group.

  2. Pay through Stripe

    The receipt Stripe emails you confirms your spot.

  3. Choose a time

Have it built

I build the thing and hand it over. Every job gets a written scope and price before any work starts.

  • Websites for local businesses

    A fast site that says what you do, when you're open and how to reach you. Built so search engines and AI assistants can read it, with structured data and an llms.txt file.

  • Small tools

    A calculator, a form, a dashboard or a booking page for one job your business does every day.

  • Automations

    Work you repeat every week, like copying data between apps or sending the same emails, set up to run on its own.

  1. You describe the job

    A few sentences on the project form is enough.

  2. I send a scope and a price

    In writing. Nothing starts until you agree to both.

  3. I build it and hand it over

    You get the code, the accounts and the domain.

Yes, with at least 24 hours of notice.

About

The Little AI Company is one person.

I'm Jeff Kazzee. I build small tools for people who use AI and intend to stay in charge of it, and I teach people to build their own. I work from rural Idaho.

Most AI products are built to take work off your hands. Some of that is useful. Past a point it takes your judgment with it, and you end up trusting output you can't inspect from a system you can't stop.

I build the other kind. A tool of mine shows you where a claim came from, keeps its state in files you can open, and waits for your approval before it changes anything that matters. It should leave you sharper than it found you.

I'm self-taught. I learned by building things with AI agents and shipping them in public, and I still work that way. When you book a session or hire me for a build, you work with me. There's no team to hand you off to.

The Little AI Club comes next: a place to learn to build with AI alongside other people. It isn't open yet.

What my tools don't do

  • No hosted memory

    I don't keep your data on my servers. I don't have servers.

  • No telemetry

    My tools don't phone home, and neither does this site.

  • No autopilot

    Nothing ships that acts on your behalf without a gate you can see.
